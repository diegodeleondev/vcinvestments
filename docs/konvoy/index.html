Konvoy Ventures

Konvoy is a Denver-based venture capital firm that invests at the early stage in interactive entertainment, deep tech and critical industries. It began as a gaming-focused fund and now carries that thesis into defense and aerospace. Official site: konvoy.vc.

Investment thesis

Konvoy invests in the technologies and platforms behind interactive entertainment. Its portfolio spans four verticals:

36 companies

Gaming & Consumer Platforms

Games, social platforms, creators, fandom and education.

17 companies

Developer Tools & Infrastructure

Analytics, version control, networking, payments and engines.

10 companies

Deep Tech & Hardware

Augmented reality, neural wearables, compute and electronic design.

7 companies

Critical Industries

Defense, aerospace, satellites, autonomous vehicles and remote operations.

Its most recent investments, such as Lux Aeterna, Seasats, Ironmist, Battle Road and several stealth companies from 2025 and 2026, apply technology born in video games (simulation, real-time compute, autonomy) to defense, aerospace and remote operations.

Frequently asked questions about Konvoy

What is Konvoy Ventures?

Konvoy Ventures is a Denver, Colorado venture capital firm that invests at the early stage in interactive entertainment, deep tech and critical industries. It started as a gaming-focused fund and now applies that thesis to defense and aerospace as well.

How big is Konvoy's latest fund?

Konvoy's most recent publicly announced fund is Konvoy Fund III, a $150 million fund announced on July 6, 2022. Third-party directories estimate total assets under management at roughly $270 million; the firm has not published that figure.

What stage does Konvoy invest in?

Konvoy invests in early-stage companies. Third-party sources place its investments between pre-seed and Series A, with checks of roughly $500K to $5M; the firm does not publish an official range.

Who founded Konvoy?

Konvoy was founded by Josh Chapman, Jason Chapman and Jackson Vaughan, who serve as Managing Partners. The investment team also includes Taylor Hurst (Partner) and Anthony Schena (Senior Associate).

Which companies are in Konvoy's portfolio?

Konvoy lists 51 companies in its portfolio, 10 of them in stealth. Well-known names include Axie Infinity (Sky Mavis), Ready Player Me, Carry1st, GameFam, Edgegap, k-ID, Genopets and Lux Aeterna.

How does Konvoy make investment decisions?

Konvoy describes itself as a thesis-driven firm that invests as a team: every opportunity goes to an investment committee, where each member's assessment is part of the decision.
